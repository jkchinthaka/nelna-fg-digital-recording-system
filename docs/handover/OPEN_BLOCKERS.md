# Open Blockers

## Executive summary

The system has extensive technical foundations, but operational handover remains blocked by business approvals, integration evidence, UAT execution, and production-go-live gates.

## Business blockers

- FG-QA-001 final content approval and publish policy
- paper-form inventory and approved-for-digitalization evidence
- official organization, site, and department values
- official shift names, codes, timings, and overnight policy
- official product catalogue and specification limits
- recorder, supervisor, and QA role mappings
- segregation-of-duties policy evidence
- applicability and effective-version business policy details
- downstream meaning of `RELEASE`, `HOLD`, and `REJECT`

## Integration blockers

- production batch source identity and contract
- Bileeta API / sandbox evidence
- external organization mapping
- ERP-side downstream authority evidence

## UAT blockers

- DEBT-01C-R-NOTO remains open for Sinhala typography evidence
- no approved published checklist content for pilot
- no approved role assignments or SoD matrix
- no hosted UAT environment decision
- device, Wi-Fi, and operational hygiene evidence incomplete
- Phase 07-10 readiness gates remain open
- UAT package remains not executed

## Production blockers

- Phase 20 UAT/pilot not passed
- no production environment recorded
- no production support owner named
- production security signoff not received
- production backup custody and RPO/RTO not approved
- paper decommission approval not received
